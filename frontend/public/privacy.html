Privacy Policy — นโยบายความเป็นส่วนตัว

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Service: MiniDraft (https://minidraft.app)

MiniDraft is an independent RoV (Arena of Valor) draft simulation and analytics web application. We value your privacy and collect only the minimum information required to run the draft workspace and optional account sync.

1. Information We Collect (ข้อมูลที่เราจัดเก็บ)

  • Guest Mode: You can use the draft board without creating an account. Draft state in Guest mode is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is not linked to an identity.
  • Account Sign-In (Username or Google Sign-In): If you sign in with Google, we request only standard openid, email, and profile scopes to receive your Google subject identifier, verified email address, and display name. We do not access your Gmail inbox, Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Saved Draft Data: When signed in, your active draft board and Global Ban series history are saved in our application database so you can sync across your devices.
  • Security & Rate Limiting: Standard HTTP session cookies (rov_session, HttpOnly) and short-lived in-memory rate-limit counters are used solely to protect login and API stability.

2. How We Use & Share Data (การใช้และการเปิดเผยข้อมูล)

  • Your account details are used strictly for authentication, session management, and cross-device sync.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email or personal data with third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion (การเก็บรักษาและขอลบข้อมูล)

Your account and synced draft history are retained while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the site administrator via the support email listed on the MiniDraft Google Sign-In consent screen.

4. Disclaimer

MiniDraft is an independent community/esports analytics tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena or TiMi Studio Group.
