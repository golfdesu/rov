Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · MiniDraft (https://minidraft.app)

MiniDraft is an independent RoV (Arena of Valor) draft simulator and analytics web app. We collect only the minimum information needed to run the draft workspace and optional account sync.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest Mode: You can use the draft board without signing in. Guest drafts stay in your browser's localStorage and are not tied to your identity.
  • Account Sign-In (Username, Google, or Discord): If you sign in with Google or Discord, we request only basic profile scopes (openid, email, and profile for Google; identity/email for Discord). We never access your messages, inbox, or files.
  • Saved Draft Data: When signed in, your draft boards, Global Ban history, and Combat Lab builds are saved so you can sync across devices.
  • Security & Rate Limiting: Standard session cookies (rov_session, HttpOnly) and short-lived rate-limit counters protect login and API stability.

2. How We Use & Share Data

  • Your account details are used strictly for sign-in, session security, and cross-device sync.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email or personal data with third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and synced draft history remain stored while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data anytime via the support email shown on the MiniDraft sign-in consent screen.

4. Disclaimer

MiniDraft is an independent community/esports analytics tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena or TiMi Studio Group.
